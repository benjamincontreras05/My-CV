# GOLDTY: Virtual Agenda & Productivity Platform

## The Problem

Physical agendas are often inefficient and make it easy to lose track of critical events. Students lacked a centralized digital tool to classify priorities and optimize their study routines.

## Process & Engineering Challenges

Built in a dynamic team. We faced communication bottlenecks, design discrepancies, and code conflicts, and resolved them through assertive mediation, clear UI patterns, and rigorous AI-assisted code reviews.

## The Solution

An interactive web platform based on time-management matrices that separates urgent, important, and non-important tasks, with tools like the Pomodoro technique to maximize focus.

tech_stack: [HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Node.js, Supabase]

# TechGuide: AI-Powered Electronic Device Assistant

## The Problem

Many people struggle to identify electronic devices and understand their purpose due to limited technological knowledge. This creates barriers to learning and interacting with everyday technology.

## The Solution

TechGuide is an AI-powered website designed to recognize electronic devices through image detection. It identifies objects and provides their names, helping users become familiar with technology and understand the devices around them.

## The Impact

By combining artificial intelligence with an accessible web interface, TechGuide makes technology easier to understand, encourages digital inclusion, and helps people develop confidence when interacting with electronic devices.

tech_stack: [HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Artificial Intelligence]
